About FreshNest

FreshNest Egg Poultry is a modern poultry farm committed to delivering high-quality, hygienic, and nutrient-rich eggs from our healthy hens. Our vision is to promote responsible farming and provide traceable, reliable products to every household and business.

Certifications

  • Certified by National Poultry Board
  • Organic & Non-GMO Feed Certification
  • HACCP-Compliant Facility

Farm Infrastructure

We maintain a state-of-the-art poultry housing system, automated egg collection, and hygienic packing unit, ensuring biosecurity and animal welfare at every step.

Farm Location

Located in the lush valleys of Pokhara, Nepal, FreshNest enjoys an optimal climate for poultry farming and proximity to local distribution channels.
