About FreshNest

Committed to Quality, Driven by Purpose

Who We Are

FreshNest Egg Poultry is a modern poultry farm based in Pokhara, Nepal. We specialize in providing hygienic, high-quality, farm-fresh eggs to households, restaurants, and distributors across the region.

Our Mission

To deliver safe, nutritious, and traceable eggs from happy hens to happy homes. We believe in transparency, welfare-focused farming, and community empowerment through sustainable agriculture.

Our Vision

To become Nepal’s most trusted egg brand through quality, customer trust, and innovative farm practices.

Why Choose Us?

  • Organic & Non-GMO Feed
  • Modern Infrastructure
  • Certified & Compliant
  • Local and Prompt Delivery
